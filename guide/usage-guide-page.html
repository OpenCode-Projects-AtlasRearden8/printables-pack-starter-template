How to Use This Pack

A plain-English guide, written like a friend would explain it.

Everything in this pack is free: free to download, free to print, free to share with anyone who needs it. There is nothing to sign up for, no email to give, and no account anywhere. You just print it and use it.

You will need

  • A printer (or a library, a school, or a friend's printer — anyone's works)
  • Plain letter-size paper, any thickness
  • Any pen or pencil you like

How to print it (about 3 minutes)

  1. Download a PDF from the output/ folder — pick whichever sheet fits your week.
  2. Open the file and click print.
  3. Set the printer scale to 100% ("Actual size"). Ignore anything that says "fit to page" — this design already fits.
  4. Print one copy. If you like how it looks, print more. If a box feels too small for your handwriting, that sentiment is correct and the design accepts your feedback.

The idea behind "recheck after" dates

Each sheet shows two dates in the header: when it was last rebuilt, and a date after which it should be re-checked against an updated version. Nothing here turns into a pumpkin — the sheet still works forever — it is just our way of being honest about when to grab a fresh copy.

Common questions

  • Can I sell prints of this? The pages themselves are MIT licensed, so yes — see the LICENSE file in the repo.
  • Do you get my email? No. There is no email collection anywhere near this project.
  • Does it work in black-and-white? Yes — every sheet is tested with color off.
  • Is this advice? No. These are paper helpers for personal use, not professional advice of any kind.
